Tap a door. Nothing here is a pitch — just the honest version, the same way Resources holds it in the hub.

What is Ringana?

An Austrian family company — skincare and supplements made fresh, toxin free, with an expiration date like food. Thirty years in. Now coming to America. Independent partners share the products; nobody here is Ringana corporate.

Is this an MLM / pyramid thing?

It’s network marketing — not a pyramid scheme. Earnings come from real product sales to real customers, not from recruiting alone. No inventory loading. Fresh products can’t sit in a garage.

Where are the products made?

Austria for now — the Fresh Factory in St. Johann. $85 million is going into a state-of-the-art Fresh Factory finishing in Virginia, so the same start-to-finish care can live here.

What does “toxin free” actually mean?

No synthetic preservatives, no microplastics, no nanoparticles, no mineral oils in the freshness story. The fresh range is vegan. They don’t test on animals. The ingredient list is meant to make sense when you read it.

Will there be monthly purchase requirements?

No monthly order requirement to stay active. You do need at least one purchase (of any size) every year to remain an active partner.

What is Evergreen Co?

A Ringana team — and a live circle. Team zooms, a person in your corner, and a private hub so you’re not piecing this together from Instagram.

Do I have to build a team?

Nope. Enjoy the products, share them, or build if you want to. The zooms, the support, and the hub are still yours. There isn’t one right way.

What is First Seeds?

The private hub for Evergreen Co: your sprout (the path), Learn (facts you can stand behind), the calendar, and Resources (your invite plus the links outside this app). The phone on this page is a walkthrough. The real rooms stay inside the team.

What is How I Grow?

A short support map so the person you sit under knows how to show up for you. It lives in the hub, on Sprout.

How much can I earn?

It depends on you. Nobody here will promise a specific income — walk away from anyone who does. What we can promise is community, strategy, and a hub that keeps the facts in one place.

When does the U.S. launch?

Partner pre-reg October 1, 2026 — $0 to reserve your spot. Products for customers November 1. Your person on the team has the exact ordering details.

What does it cost to start?

Pre-registration on October 1 is $0. That’s just your partner spot. A Founder Pack is optional — about $220. You don’t have to buy everything on day one.

What if I just want the products?

Perfect. Tell the person who sent you this. They’ll follow up with launch timing. No requirement to share.

How do I actually join?

Go back to whoever sent you this page — text them, DM them, ask the next question. That’s your person. If they have a First Seeds invite, that’s how you walk into the hub.
