How one simple YES can

Nine years ago I said yes to an opportunity that changed my life, my family’s life, and countless lives around the country. Now I get to say YES again — and I couldn’t be more excited.

The Short Version

Hi, I'm Kim!

I’m a homeschooling, home-birthing mom to four beautiful kiddos under 10, married to my best friend, doing my best to live a life filled with gratitude for all the beautiful things God’s given me. Find me on Instagram at @Drkimberlyjoy.

As a chiropractor, I’ve always had a wild passion for all things health and wellness. In 2016, God gave me an opportunity to expand my reach and help families all around the country choose safe products for their bodies and their homes.

It brings me so much joy to help families not just survive, but THRIVE!

That First Yes

Direct sales wasn’t on my radar. At all.

Before I knew it, my simple “yes” led to building one of the largest organizations in the clean beauty direct sales industry. And I was MIND-BLOWN.

It was never something I thought I’d do, but God had other plans. And because I said yes, even when it was scary, I’ve had the privilege of impacting countless families over the last decade.

There is truly so much joy and fulfillment in helping families learn how to choose safe products, because this life is so much better when our families are thriving!

The Journey

And now the story continues…

A passion for wellness

As a chiropractor, I’ve always cared deeply about what goes in and on our bodies. Helping families choose safer products became a natural extension of that calling — and then a full-blown mission.

02

The yes that changed everything

In 2016, God opened a door I never expected. Saying yes — even when it was scary — led to impacting countless families and building one of the largest organizations in clean beauty direct sales.

03

Why Ringana, why now?

Ringana is a family-founded Austrian company that’s been making fresh, totally toxin-free skincare, body care, and supplements since 1996. Fresh production. Full-potency plant actives. Transparency you can trust. A business model that allows me to help people, WHILE supporting my family. It’s the standard I found in 2016 that first made me fall in love with direct sales… and this is the first time I’ve found it again a decade later. What a blessing to be given another opportunity to help people around the world while providing for my family!

04

What’s next

I’m gathering a founding community before the U.S. launch. Skin perfection, deodorant, baby care, CAPS cerebro, and the ABC packs are at the top of my try-first list — and I’ll share honest notes as I go. If you’ve trusted my recommendations before, I’d love for you to be here for this chapter too.

The Right Fit

It’s important to me that I feel like the right fit for you.

I'd rather have ten people who are genuinely aligned than a hundred who aren't. This business only works when it's built on real trust.

I'm not here to pressure anyone. I'm here to share what I've found, answer your questions honestly, and help you decide if this is right for your life — whether that's as a customer, a teammate, or neither.

If you care about ingredient transparency, products that actually perform, and building something meaningful before a market even opens — we should talk. If you're looking for a get-rich-quick scheme, I'm probably not your person, and that's okay.

The best partnerships start with honesty on both sides. That's how I've always operated, and it's not changing now.
