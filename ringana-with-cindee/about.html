Where It Began

My sister's cancer first got my attention.

Her diagnosis caused me to begin looking more closely at the choices I was making for my own health. I started with food. I wasn't surprised to learn that so much of what I was eating wasn't good for me. Little by little, I began eliminating processed, chemical-filled foods, replacing them with healthy proteins, vegetables, and fruits.

Then I began researching the products I used in our home. I discovered many of the household cleaners I had trusted weren't safe. That didn't entirely surprise me either.

But when I learned about the ingredients in my makeup, skincare, and body care, I was stunned.

And sickened. Literally.

Body · Mind · Soul

God created us as whole people.

That discovery became a turning point in my own health journey. It also became the place where God began growing a passion within me—not only to make different choices for myself, but to help others learn how to reduce the toxic burdens affecting their bodies, minds and souls.

Over time, I began to see that wellness involves so much more than what we eat or put on our skin. God created us as whole people. What affects the body can affect the mind and soul, and what we carry mentally and spiritually can affect the way we care for our bodies.

As this body • mind • soul vision continued to unfold, I began asking God a simple question:

"What's next?"

RINGANA

Very soon after that prayer, a friend shared RINGANA with me.

I immediately experienced a stirring in my spirit that this might be an answer to what I had been asking God. The company's commitment to freshness, thoughtful formulation, and whole-person wellness aligned so naturally with the vision He had already been growing in my heart.

I couldn't wait to know more.

As I began experiencing the products for myself, that initial stirring became an unmistakable sense that this was right.

I learned a long time ago not to try to outrun God. When He brings something into my life that aligns so clearly with the path He has already been preparing, I want to have the courage to follow—even when it leads somewhere new and unknown.

Because for me, RINGANA is about far more than products. It is an opportunity to help others live with greater intention, make more informed choices, and care for the whole person God created us to be.

What began with my late sister's cancer and continued with my own health struggles has become a passion, a calling, and now a beautiful new opportunity.

Only God can take something initiated through heartbreak and allow it to become a meaningful purpose.
