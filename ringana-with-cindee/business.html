How It Works

A business that stays simple.

Because Ringana products are made fresh to order, they can't sit in stores — or in your garage. You recommend them. Ringana handles the rest. Share samples if you choose.

01

You share what you genuinely use

Your role is recommendation, not sales-floor pressure. You use the products, you talk about them honestly, and you point people to your personal online shop link. That's the whole front end of the business.

02

Ringana handles everything behind the scenes

Orders go through your personal online office, and Ringana produces fresh, packages, ships direct to your customer, and handles invoicing. No inventory, no packing boxes, no chasing payments.

03

You earn on real product sales

Commission comes from products your customers actually order and reorder — and from supporting a team of partners you help build, if you choose to grow one. The engine is product sales, not recruiting.

04

You grow with a mentor, at your pace

Every new partner starts with a mentor — that's where I come in. Training, tools, and systems from day one, whether you want a few hours a week alongside your family or a full-time business.

Questions

A few questions I hear often.

What if I just want the products?

That's a lovely way to start. I'll share product info with everyone who signs up. I'd also invite you to join while RINGANA is coming to market, in case you want to share later. There's no requirement to share once you join, so you can decide in time.

Is this a pyramid scheme?

No. It's network marketing, built on products people actually use and reorder. A pyramid scheme pays people mainly for recruiting, with little or no real product behind it. With RINGANA, earnings come from product sales to real customers—not from recruitment alone. There's no inventory loading, and because the products are made fresh, they can't sit in a garage.

What does it cost to get started?

Official U.S. enrollment details will be confirmed at launch. I'll share them with the founding list the moment they're public.

Do I have to buy every month to stay active?

No. There are no monthly purchase requirements. You do need at least one purchase, of any size, once a year to remain an active partner.

How much can I earn?

It depends entirely on you. Partners earn commission on product sales and on team growth, and results vary widely. Nobody—including me—can promise you a specific income. If anyone does, that's a good reason to walk away. What I can offer is my systems, my mentorship, and founding-team timing.

Do I have to be a salesperson?

No. That energy doesn't fit these products anyway. You use them, you share your real experience, and RINGANA handles the rest. If you've ever told a friend about something you love, you already know how to do this.

When does RINGANA open in America?

The American facility in Roanoke, Virginia begins manufacturing in late 2026, with the U.S. market opening around that time. Exact partner enrollment and ordering dates will come from RINGANA—and founding list members will hear them from me first.
