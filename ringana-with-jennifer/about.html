About Me

Covid changed everything — and so did the next pivot.

A decade in healthcare. Six years educating moms about non-toxic living. Capitol Hill. Bankruptcy. And a brand that finally meets the standard I ask of every product I recommend.

The Short Version

Hi, I'm Jen — I'm so glad you're here!

I'm a mom to two incredible little boys, a wife to a restaurant entrepreneur, and a non-toxic mama who started with a decade in healthcare as a Dermatology Surgery RN, BSN. You can find me at @thejenniferruhling.

Motherhood completely transformed my outlook on product safety. I became an avid label reader, obsessing over the health and safety of my family — which led me to leave healthcare and build a personal brand educating other moms about non-toxic living.

Over the last six years I've had the privilege of turning that education into a career — from lobbying on Capitol Hill for safer beauty laws in April 2022 to curating non-toxic education for a growing audience. Helping others live healthier lives is the work I was meant to do.

Then Covid — and later, an industry collapse — asked me to pivot again.

Where It Shifted

Covid changed everything.

In 2019 I exited healthcare to become a stay-at-home mom so that my husband could solely focus on our family's restaurant, bar, and event business. My job became maintaining the home front. Then Covid entered the scene and changed everything.

Overnight we went from a clear vision as a family — both financially and professionally — into crisis.

As my husband went to work daily to keep the business afloat during a forced government shutdown, I started to wonder what I could do to earn income without going back to nursing.

With a robust knowledge base of skin and a passion for raising my family in a non-toxic environment, I knew I could pivot my skills to social media. If others could do it, why couldn't I?

So I started. Passionately. With education at the center.

I began promoting a clean beauty brand suggested by my sister. I became deeply invested in selling these products with the primary focus of educating women about the lack of regulation of personal care products in the United States. This brand offered a solution — products that both performed and omitted a long list of ingredients known to harm human health.

In 2024 I earned #7 in the company in personal sales in an incentive opportunity to lobby again on Capitol Hill. Our mission was to help close the fragrance loophole and tighten regulation around hiding ingredients under the umbrella term "fragrance."

Two days before leaving for Washington, D.C., the company — valued just two years before at $1 billion — declared bankruptcy. This was largely due to majority shareholder private equity, The Carlyle Group, making a slew of poor investments and misdirection of the brand.

Yet Another Pivot

We plan and God laughs.

With the closing of my former clean beauty company, I was driven to grow my Instagram presence, my personal brand, and find a new skincare and makeup brand that superseded expectations from the former company.

Those two years gave me an amazing opportunity to keep learning about ingredients, expand what I could offer my audience and teammates, and continue building relationships — impacting other women's lives both professionally in mentoring and leadership, and as an expert in the industry.

That search led me to Ringana — a family-founded Austrian company making fresh, totally toxin-free skincare and supplements since 1996. Fresh production instead of preservation. Full-potency plant actives. An in-house campus. Transparency you can actually read.

This is the standard I've been asking the industry to meet — and the brand I'm choosing to build with next.

Why Build With Me

Why build with me?

I've spent six years turning non-toxic education into a career — clinical skin knowledge from nursing, Capitol Hill advocacy for safer beauty laws, and a platform built on helping moms make clearer choices for their families.

When you join my founding team, you get that foundation: ingredient fluency you can trust, mentorship from someone who's already led through pivots, and a partner who puts education before hype.

The Right Fit

It's important to me that I feel like the right fit for you.

I'd rather have ten people who are genuinely aligned than a hundred who aren't. This business only works when it's built on real trust.

I'm not here to pressure anyone. I'm here to share what I've found, answer your questions honestly, and help you decide if this is right for your life — whether that's as a customer, a teammate, or neither.

If you care about ingredient transparency, products that actually perform, and building something meaningful before a market even opens — we should talk. If you're looking for a get-rich-quick scheme, I'm probably not your person, and that's okay.

The best partnerships start with honesty on both sides. That's how I've always operated, and it's not changing now.
