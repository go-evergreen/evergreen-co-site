The Mission

"We can do better than that."

That sentence has guided Ringana for thirty years: prove that skincare and supplements can be completely fresh, genuinely effective, and free of every unnecessary additive — and that a company can be transparent while doing it.

Every formula starts from scratch, and because Ringana develops, produces, and ships from its own facilities, there's no gap between what they promise and what you receive.

Three values have never changed: freshness, sustainability, and transparency. Everything on this page flows from those three.

The Standard

Toxin free that means toxin free.

Most "clean" brands remove a few headline offenders and call it a day. Ringana was built the opposite way: begin with nothing, and only add what serves your skin and body.

Every ingredient is vetted for naturalness and sustainability before it's allowed near a formula — with transparent supply chains and regular controls behind the claims.

Because every product is made fresh and shipped directly, there's no need for the artificial scaffolding that props up conventional formulas sitting on shelves for years. What replaces all of it? Concentrated plant actives — antioxidants and sensitive botanical ingredients so potent they couldn't survive in a conventional, shelf-stable formula at all.

The System

Four lines, built to live together.

From your morning serum to your evening supplement, the whole routine comes from one place, held to one bar. Here's how it fits together.

Skincare · Body · Men's

FRESH is the heart of Ringana — certified fresh skincare covering your full routine, from cleansing and serums to moisture, eye care, body care, deodorant, and men's care. Formulas are built on highly concentrated antioxidant plant actives, the kind of sensitive ingredients that degrade in conventional products but arrive at full potency when made fresh.

Targeted Supplements

CAPS deliver concentrated plant power in capsule form, each blend designed for a specific need. They're the "meet your body where it is" layer of the system — built to slot into your daily rhythm alongside FRESH and PACKS.

Daily Foundation

PACKS are portion-sized sachets of powdered plant nutrition — the consistent foundation underneath everything else. The ABC trio covers three daily needs: antioxidant support, acid–base balance, and digestive fibre.

Functional Beverages

DRINKS are clean functional shots and beverages — real support without the artificial sweeteners, colors, and mystery ingredients that dominate the beverage aisle.

Manufacturing Standards

Nothing outsourced. Nothing assumed.

Most beauty and supplement brands are marketing companies — the actual formulating and manufacturing happens at contract facilities they don't control. Ringana is the opposite: a manufacturer first.

Developed by an in-house research team

Every formula is conceived by Ringana's own scientists — not licensed, not white-labeled, not copied from a contract lab's catalog. The complex recipes are theirs alone.

Produced on Ringana's own campus

Products are crafted in small batches at the company's own facilities in Styria, Austria — from the original Frischewerk to the modern campus at St. Johann in der Haide. Soon, the Roanoke, Virginia facility brings that same in-house production to American soil.

Made fresh, to order

Small-batch, on-demand production means every order is made close to the moment you place it — never pulled from a warehouse where it's been aging under fluorescent lights. Logistics, too, run through Ringana's own center.

Vetted suppliers, audited chains

Raw ingredients come from carefully selected suppliers who meet Ringana's criteria for naturalness and sustainability, with transparent supply chains and regular controls — all documented publicly in the company's transparency report.

Why Fresh Matters

The expiration date is the proof.

Ringana products expire in months, not years — like real food. That short date is the receipt: proof that nothing artificial is propping the formula up on a shelf.

Full-potency actives

The plant compounds that do the most for your skin — antioxidants, vitamins — are also the fastest to degrade. Fresh production means they arrive working, not faded.

Nothing propping it up

A three-year shelf life requires artificial preservation. A fresh product simply doesn't. The short date is proof that nothing synthetic is holding the formula together.

Made to order, shipped direct

Small batches produced on demand and sent straight to your door — never warehoused for months under fluorescent lights. Soon, made fresh on American soil in Virginia.

The Whole Picture

Clean for your body. Clean for the planet.

Toxin free doesn't stop at the ingredient list. Ringana's operations are built around the same principle: leave nothing behind that doesn't belong.

The REuse program: first of its kind

Ringana was the first company in the cosmetics industry to make its glass flakons genuinely refillable. Return ten empty glass flakons and you receive a free product of your choice. Returned bottles are inspected by hand, sterilized in a laboratory-grade washing process, fitted with all-new inner pouches and pumps, and sent back into production for a second life.

Packaging built to reduce waste

Ringana isn't plastic-free — and they don't claim to be. Skincare ships in refillable glass flakons (with new inner pouches and pumps on each cycle), supplements use what's needed for safety and freshness, and shipping relies on compostable corn-starch packing chips made in-house, paper tape, and recycled fiber cartons. They're also working toward 30% post-consumer recycled plastic in packaging by 2030 — with about 20% PCR content already in use.

On-demand production

Ringana produces in small batches based on actual orders — not warehouse forecasts. That means less overproduction, less waste sitting on shelves, and products that reach you closer to the moment they were made.

Transparency you can read for yourself

Ringana publishes public transparency and sustainability reports covering ingredients, sourcing, packaging, and the business model — plus detailed "Care Facts" on individual products. It's more disclosure than most beauty brands offer, even if it's still the company's own reporting.

Everything under one roof

Development, production, and logistics all happen on Ringana's own campus — full control of quality from raw ingredient to your doorstep, with no middlemen in between.

Measured results, not just promises

Ringana's own FRESH IMPACT reporting tracks concrete numbers: a 37% REuse rate on eligible glass flakons, 76% internal recycling across operations, and ongoing investment in circular packaging — including a climate partnership protecting the Dürrenstein-Lassingtal wilderness in Austria.

On My Radar

What I'm most looking forward to trying.

The full U.S. lineup is still being finalized, but through my partnership with Ringana I'm getting early access before launch. These seven are where I'm starting — my honest firsts, not the whole catalog. Join the list and you'll hear my take as I work through them.

FRESH · Deodorant

FRESH deodorant is a nourishing water-in-oil emulsion that reliably protects against odor without aluminium salts. Rice husk water, baking soda, and zinc oxide deliver long-lasting freshness, while shea butter and oat oil intensively care for sensitive underarm skin — so you're protecting and moisturising in the same step.

FRESH · Body Care

FRESH body wash is a microbiome-friendly shower gel that cleanses gently while protecting the skin's natural balance. Moisturising extracts of moss, polypody fern, and witch hazel leave skin smooth, refreshed, and cared for — ideal for sensitive skin and everyday family use.

FRESH · Hair Care

FRESH repair shampoo is built for damaged, coloured, or dry hair. An innovative blend of fermented rice water, five precious mushroom extracts — including reishi and chaga — plus vegan proteins and arginine helps repair hair from within, strengthen structure, and restore softness and natural shine.

BEYOND · Omega-3

BEYOND omega delivers plant-based EPA and DHA from sustainable algae oil — vegan omega-3 support for heart, brain, and focus without fish or typical marine contaminants. The formula goes beyond a basic omega-3: it's completed with natural carotenoids, ubiquinol (coenzyme Q10), and vitamins D3, K2, and E so the fat-soluble nutrients arrive as a thoughtful daily foundation.

BEYOND · Gut & Microbiome

BEYOND biotic supports digestive health with over 21 billion live microorganisms per daily dose from ten probiotic strains. The formula is paired with riboflavin, vitamin D3, and vitamin B12 to promote wellbeing while strengthening your natural gut flora — microbiome support you can feel as balance and lightness from within.

CAPS · Joints & Mobility

CAPS move supports joint health and natural freedom of movement with a carefully selected blend of plant extracts and vitamins that help strengthen bones and muscles — whether you train regularly or simply want to move through everyday life with more ease.

DRINKS · Energy

RINGANAchi is Ringana's plant-based energy shot — a caffeinated pick-me-up with a fruity, spicy note designed for gentle, long-lasting energy instead of the spike-and-crash cycle of coffee or conventional energy drinks.
