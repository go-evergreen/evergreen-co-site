Every formula starts from scratch, and because Ringana develops, produces, and ships from its own facilities, there's no gap between what they promise and what you receive.

Their three pillars are actually: Freshness, Trendsetting and Excellence

Manufacturing Standards

Nothing outsourced. Nothing assumed.

Most beauty and supplement brands are marketing companies — the actual formulating and manufacturing happens at contract facilities they don't control. Ringana is the opposite: a manufacturer first.

Developed by an in-house research team

Every formula is conceived by Ringana's own scientists — not licensed, not white-labeled, not copied from a contract lab's catalog. The complex recipes are theirs alone.

Produced on Ringana's own campus

Products are crafted in small batches at the company's own facilities in Styria, Austria — from the original Frischewerk to the modern campus at St. Johann in der Haide. Soon, the Roanoke, Virginia facility brings that same in-house production to American soil.

Made fresh, to order

Small-batch, on-demand production means every order is made close to the moment you place it — never pulled from a warehouse where it's been aging under fluorescent lights. Logistics, too, run through Ringana's own center.

Vetted suppliers, audited chains

Raw ingredients come from carefully selected suppliers who meet Ringana's criteria for naturalness and sustainability, with transparent supply chains and regular controls — all documented publicly in the company's transparency report.

Why Fresh Matters

The expiration date is proof.

Ringana products expire in months, not years — like real food. The short date is something they’re proud of.

Full-potency actives

The plant compounds that do the most for your skin — antioxidants, vitamins — are also the fastest to degrade. Fresh production means they arrive working, not faded.

Made to order, shipped direct

Small batches produced on demand and sent straight to your door — never warehoused for months under fluorescent lights. Soon, made fresh on American soil in Virginia.

The Whole Picture

Clean for your body. Clean for the planet.

Toxin free doesn't stop at the ingredient list. Ringana's operations are built around the same principle: leave nothing behind that doesn't belong.

The REuse program: first of its kind

Ringana was the first company in the cosmetics industry to make its glass flakons genuinely refillable. Return ten empty glass flakons and you receive a free product of your choice. Returned bottles are inspected by hand, sterilized in a laboratory-grade washing process, fitted with all-new inner pouches and pumps, and sent back into production for a second life.

Packaging built to reduce waste

Ringana isn't plastic-free — and they don't claim to be. Skincare ships in refillable glass flakons (with new inner pouches and pumps on each cycle), supplements use what's needed for safety and freshness, and shipping relies on compostable corn-starch packing chips made in-house, paper tape, and recycled fiber cartons. They're also working toward 30% post-consumer recycled plastic in packaging by 2030 — with about 20% PCR content already in use.

On-demand production

Ringana produces in small batches based on actual orders — not warehouse forecasts. That means less overproduction, less waste sitting on shelves, and products that reach you closer to the moment they were made.

Transparency you can read for yourself

Ringana publishes public transparency and sustainability reports covering ingredients, sourcing, packaging, and the business model — plus detailed "Care Facts" on individual products. It's more disclosure than most beauty brands offer, even if it's still the company's own reporting.

Everything under one roof

Development, production, and logistics all happen on Ringana's own campus — full control of quality from raw ingredient to your doorstep, with no middlemen in between.

Measured results, not just promises

Ringana's own FRESH IMPACT reporting tracks concrete numbers: a 37% REuse rate on eligible glass flakons, 76% internal recycling across operations, and ongoing investment in circular packaging — including a climate partnership protecting the Dürrenstein-Lassingtal wilderness in Austria.
