About Me

Decades of label reading lead me here.

I've helped thousands of people navigate cleaner beauty and healthier choices. Every experience sharpened my understanding of ingredients, formulation, and transparency - and ultimately led me to a company whose philosophy feels deeply aligned with my own.

The Short Version

Hi, I'm Lily. I'm so glad you're here!

I'm a mom to two incredible boys, a lifelong label reader, clean beauty educator, and someone who genuinely believes people deserve better information before making decisions about the products they use every day.

Long before "clean beauty" became trendy, I was the girl flipping over bottles to read ingredient labels. I grew up in a holistic family where we talked about ingredients, nutrition, and wellness around the dinner table. My parents taught me to ask questions, think critically, and understand that what we put in and on our bodies matters. Looking back, it's no surprise that ingredient labels became my love language.

For the last decade, I've had the privilege of turning that passion into my career.

Where I Started

A leap of faith that changed everything.

I began in direct sales in 2014 during a season when my husband was in chiropractic school and we were raising two little boys on student loans and food assistance. What started as a leap of faith to help our family financially ended up changing the course of our lives.

Since then, I've spent a decade building a clean beauty organization from the ground up — entirely without an active upline or established systems. What I'm most proud of isn't the size of it. It's the trust: people who know I won't recommend anything I wouldn't use on my own family.

How I Recommend

Trust takes time. Products have to earn it.

If you've followed me for any length of time, you already know something about me: I don't have a new favorite product every week. I don't recommend products after using them for three days. And I don't believe every "clean" brand deserves the title.

Over the years, I've watched the wellness industry grow tremendously. That's exciting — but it's also made me more discerning. Although I’ve found a handful of brands that I feel very aligned with sharing… I found myself still wishing more companies would raise their standards. More transparency. More innovation. More integrity. More willingness to do things differently.

At the same time, I was reflecting on my growth as an individual. After spending nearly ten years building a large organization — entirely without an active upline or established systems — I realized I wasn't simply looking for another product to share. I was looking for a long-term vision inspired me and felt timeless.
